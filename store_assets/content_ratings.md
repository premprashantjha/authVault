## Justification for Ratings

### Why PEGI 3 / Everyone / 4+?

1. **No Violence:** The app contains no violent content
2. **No Sexual Content:** The app contains no sexual or suggestive content
3. **No Profanity:** The app uses professional, neutral language
4. **No Substances:** No references to alcohol, tobacco, or drugs
5. **No Gambling:** No gambling mechanics or features
6. **No Scary Content:** No horror or frightening elements
7. **No User Interaction:** No social features or user-generated content
8. **Educational Value:** Teaches users about two-factor authentication security
9. **Utility Purpose:** Simple tool for generating authentication codes
10. **Privacy Focused:** No data collection or tracking

### Why Not Lower (0+/3+)?

The 4+ rating on Apple is the lowest available rating for apps. PEGI 3 is the equivalent lowest rating on Google Play.

### Why Not Higher?

There is no content that would warrant a higher age rating. The app is purely functional with no potentially inappropriate content.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Why do I need a content rating?**
A: Both Google Play and Apple App Store require content ratings for all apps. It helps users make informed decisions.

**Q: Can I skip the questionnaire?**
A: No, content rating is mandatory for app publication.

**Q: What if I answer incorrectly?**
A: You can retake the questionnaire. However, intentionally providing false information can result in app removal.

**Q: How long does rating approval take?**
A: Usually instant on Google Play (IARC is automated). Apple reviews as part of app review (1-3 days).

**Q: Do I need to pay for ratings?**
A: No, content ratings are free on both platforms.

**Q: What if my app is rejected due to rating?**
A: This is unlikely for a utility app with no concerning content. If it happens, review and correct your answers.
